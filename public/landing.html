Maps for Liberty County.

Navigation built for ER:LC. See live players and calls on the map, get turn-by-turn directions, and find the nearest hospital, police or fire station.

Everything on one map.

Made for roleplay: it keeps up with your server instead of guessing.

📍

Live players

Everyone on your server appears on the map, color-coded by team. Your own car follows you as you drive.

🧭

Turn-by-turn

Search a postal, street, building or player and get directions with distances in feet and miles. Leave the route and it reroutes.

🚨

Calls on the map

Emergency and mod calls appear as pins. Tap one to route straight to it.

🏥

Nearest help

One tap finds the closest hospital, police, sheriff, fire station, bank, gas station and more.

🏙️

2D and 3D

A drawn Apple-style map with raised buildings. Switch between flat and tilted any time.

🖥️

Phone, browser, desktop

Add it to your home screen, or use the Windows app as an overlay on top of Roblox.
